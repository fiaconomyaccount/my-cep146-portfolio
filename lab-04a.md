## Exercise 2: Recipe Card

# Blueberry Pancakes

## Ingredients
1. 1/2 cup whole wheat and all purpose flour
2. 2 table spoons of honey and maple syrup
3. 1 teaspoon baking powder
4. 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
5. 1/4 teaspoon salt
6. 3/4 cup milk
7. 1 large egg
8. 2 tablespoons melted butter
9. 1/2 cup fresh blueberries

## Cooking Steps
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, mix the milk, egg, and melted butter together.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined. **Tip: Do not overmix the batter; small lumps are perfectly fine.**
4. Gently fold in the fresh blueberries.
5. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and grease lightly with butter or cooking spray.
6. Pour 1/4 cup of batter onto the skillet for each pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and then flip.
7. **Tip: Flip only once when the edges look dry and bubbles pop, cooking for another 1–2 minutes until golden brown.**

## Exercise 3: Academic Integrity Policy Document

# Seneca Academic Integrity Policy Summary

## Purpose & Key Definitions
The purpose of this policy is to maintain a high standard of academic integrity across the Seneca community.
> **Academic Integrity** is the expectation to act with honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility, and courage.

### Core Policy Violations
* **Plagiarism:** Presenting work done by others as your own.
* **Cheating:** Engaging in dishonest practices during evaluations or assessments.
* **Falsification:** Altering or inventing information, data, or citations.
* **Impersonation:** Falsely acting as another student for academic evaluations.
* **Contract Cheating:** Paying or commissioning an external service to do your schoolwork.

### Appendix A: Reporting and Procedures Timeline

| Action Required | Deadline / Timeline | Responsible Party |
